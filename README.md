# Breaking Bad
### A Television Series Review

## A. Introduction

**Breaking Bad** is an American television series created by Vince Gilligan. It premiered on January 20, 2008, and follows the life of Walter White, a high school chemistry teacher who turns to producing illegal drugs after receiving a serious cancer diagnosis. As the story progresses, his decisions transform him from an ordinary teacher into a powerful figure in the drug trade.

The series combines **crime, drama, and thriller** elements. It is widely recognized for its compelling storytelling, character development, and unexpected plot twists.

> A story about how one man's choices can completely change his life.

## B. Synopsis

The story follows Walter White, a high school chemistry teacher living in Albuquerque, New Mexico. After discovering that he has lung cancer, Walter becomes worried about how his family will survive financially after his death.

To secure his family's future, he teams up with his former student, Jesse Pinkman, to manufacture and sell methamphetamine. Using his chemistry knowledge, Walter creates an unusually pure product that quickly attracts attention in the criminal world.

As their business grows, Walter becomes involved with dangerous criminals and faces increasingly difficult situations. He begins using the name "Heisenberg" and gradually becomes more ruthless and ambitious.

Meanwhile, his family remains unaware of the full extent of his criminal activities. His brother-in-law, Hank Schrader, works for the Drug Enforcement Administration (DEA), creating additional tension as he investigates the drug trade.

Throughout the series, Walter's decisions affect everyone around him, leading to betrayal, conflict, and tragic consequences. The story explores the effects of greed, power, pride, and the choices people make.

## C. Characters

1. **Walter White (Heisenberg)** – A chemistry teacher who begins manufacturing methamphetamine after being diagnosed with cancer. He gradually becomes a powerful and feared criminal.

2. **Jesse Pinkman** – Walter's former student and business partner. He helps Walter manufacture and sell drugs but struggles with guilt and the consequences of their actions.

3. **Skyler White** – Walter's wife who becomes suspicious of his unusual behavior and eventually discovers the truth about his criminal activities.

4. **Hank Schrader** – Walter's brother-in-law and a DEA agent who investigates the illegal drug trade without initially knowing Walter's secret identity.

5. **Saul Goodman** – A clever and dishonest lawyer who helps Walter and Jesse deal with legal problems and criminal situations.

## D. Personal Review

I chose **Breaking Bad** because it has an interesting storyline and shows how a person's decisions can completely change their life. I also enjoy stories that feature intelligent characters, suspense, and unexpected events.

### Three Things I Like About the Series

- **Character Development:** Walter White's transformation throughout the series is fascinating. His personality and decisions change significantly as the story progresses.
- **Storytelling:** The series keeps viewers interested through its suspense, conflicts, and unexpected plot twists.
- **Acting and Production:** The performances, cinematography, and background music make the scenes more emotional and memorable.

### Personal Rating

**My Rating: 10/10**

I would give Breaking Bad a 10 out of 10 because of its excellent storytelling, memorable characters, and strong character development.

### Recommendation

I highly recommend Breaking Bad to people who enjoy crime, drama, and thriller television series. It is especially worth watching for those who appreciate complex characters and stories with meaningful consequences. However, viewers should be aware that the series contains mature themes, violence, and illegal activities.
